Our School Mission

Excellence for every student

Make excellence a predictable outcome for every student in the school by rooting their education firmly in the matrix of Islamic concepts and a strong technology-based curriculum. We prepare them with 21st-century skills and faith-based values, generating the intellectual energy and productivity needed to overcome academic and professional challenges and be successful citizens.

The Islamic concept of “ilm”

Integrating the pursuit of knowledge with values and promoting balance and genuine synthesis.

Excellence in learning

Making the excellence of the Islamic heritage the foundation of a rigorous academic program.

STEM foundation

Early exploration of Science, Technology, Engineering & Mathematics fields.
