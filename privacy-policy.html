How we protect your information

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use our service, and tells you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

Last updated: March 12, 2023

We use your personal data to provide and improve the service. By using the service you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Interpretation and Definitions

Interpretation

The words of which the initial letter is capitalised have meanings defined under the following conditions. The following definitions shall have the same meaning regardless of whether they appear in singular or in plural.

Definitions

  • Account means a unique account created to access our service or parts of it.
  • Affiliate means an entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party.
  • Company (referred to as “We”, “Us” or “Our”) refers to Al Manara Academy and Mosque, 929 Big Bend Road, Ballwin, Missouri 63021.
  • Cookies are small files placed on your device that contain details of your browsing history on a website.
  • Country refers to: Missouri, United States.
  • Device means any device that can access the service.
  • Personal Data is any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Service refers to the website or the application, or both.
  • Service Provider means a third party that processes data on behalf of the Company.
  • Usage Data is data collected automatically, generated by the use of the service.
  • Website refers to Al Manara Academy and Mosque, accessible from www.masjidalmanara.com.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data. While using the service we may ask for information that can be used to contact or identify you, including your email address and usage data.

Usage Data. Usage data is collected automatically and may include your device Internet Protocol address, browser type and version, the pages you visit, time and date of visits, time spent on pages, unique device identifiers, and other diagnostic data.

Tracking Technologies and Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity and store certain information, including browser cookies and web beacons. Cookies can be “persistent” or “session” cookies. We use essential cookies to provide the service, cookie-notice acceptance cookies, and functionality cookies to remember your choices.

Use of Your Personal Data

  • To provide and maintain the service, including monitoring usage.
  • To manage your account and registration.
  • For the performance of a contract (for example, tuition).
  • To contact you with updates or informative communications.
  • To manage your requests and to evaluate and improve the service.
  • For business transfers, data analysis, and promotional communications when you have not opted out.

We may share personal information with service providers, affiliates, business partners, in the event of a business transfer, with other users when you interact in public areas, or with your consent.

Retention of Your Personal Data

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this policy, to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Usage data is retained for a shorter period unless needed for security or legal purposes.

Transfer of Your Personal Data

Your information may be transferred to and processed in places outside your state, province or country where data protection laws may differ. We take reasonable steps to keep your data treated securely, and transfers are only made where adequate controls exist.

Delete Your Personal Data

You have the right to delete data we hold about you, or to ask us to help you delete it. You may update, amend, or delete information at any time, and contact us to request access, correction, or deletion. We may need to retain certain information where legally required.

Disclosure of Your Personal Data

Business Transactions

If the Company is involved in a merger, acquisition or asset sale, your personal data may be transferred with advance notice and a different Privacy Policy.

Law enforcement

We may disclose data if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

Other legal requirements

  • Comply with a legal obligation
  • Protect and defend the rights or property of the Company
  • Prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing
  • Protect the personal safety of users or the public
  • Protect against legal liability

Security of Your Personal Data

Security matters to us, but no method of transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. We use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, yet cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from under-13s. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will remove it from our servers.

Links to Other Websites

Our service may link to third-party websites who operate their own policies. We encourage you to review the policy of every site you visit.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes, update the “Last updated” date, and you are encouraged to review this page periodically.
